##  🐳🐳🐳 **Yuwen Chen (陈渝文)**
(Member, IEEE) received the MS degree in computer software and theory from Zhengzhou University, Zhengzhou, China, in 2015, and the PhD degree in telematic engineering form the Technical University of Madrid, Madrid, Spain, in 2019. He is an associate professor with the School of Computer Science, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China. His research interests include IoT security and privacy, collaborative learning, model inversion, and gradient inversion.<br>
陈渝文，副教授，北京工业大学计算机学院。长期专注于物联网数据安全与隐私保护领域的研究工作，主要研究方向为物联网环境中的人工智能安全技术，尤其在图像隐私保护的攻防方面有深入研究积累，具体涵盖图像梯度反演攻击、模型反演攻击等关键前沿问题。
